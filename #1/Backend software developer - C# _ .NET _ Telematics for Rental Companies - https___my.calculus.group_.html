Job description

  • You develop, maintain, test and evaluate microservices.
  • You develop high-performance and scalable microservices.
  • You make secure REST APIs available to external customers.
  • You detect potential performance bottlenecks or security problems and improve them.
  • You actively follow trends and developments in your field and you like to share that knowledge with your colleagues.

Profile

About you

  • You have a degree in technology or computer science (or equivalent through experience).
  • You have extensive experience with C#, .NET, Entity Framework and SQL.
  • You have an eye for detail.
  • You are creative and solution-oriented
  • You have strong analytical skills.
  • You have a first experience.
  • You keep security in mind at every step.
  • Experiences with Service Fabric and Angular (version 2+) are a plus.
  • You are interested in new technologies and a tutorial is sufficient. Also, you don't need checkmarks to find good answers on Stackoverflow. In short: you like to learn out of your own interest and you are not afraid of the unknown.
  • You can critically evaluate when yet another buzzword or fanboy passes.
  • You like autonomy and responsibility in project work.
  • You are able to analyse, understand and prioritize problems.
  • You are stress resistant.

Over Calculus

Calculus develops and produces high-quality IoT software and hardware. The combination of our own gateways and our universal cloud platform ensures that our customers can monitor and digitize their installations in a user-friendly way. In addition to data capture and monitoring, we also offer a solution for remote control. Innovation and inspiration are at the heart of our informal work culture.

The advantages

Besides the afternoon walks, Wednesday fries and Friday drinks, we also offer you the following:

  • A challenging, versatile job in which innovation is paramount.
  • The opportunity to work behind the scenes at major events and festivals.
  • Career opportunities in a motivated team of talented employees.
  • An informal working environment where responsibility and teamwork are important.
  • Sliding hours with respect for private.
  • A permanent contract with full-time employment.
  • A competitive salary package with fringe benefits such as meal vouchers, ADV days,...
